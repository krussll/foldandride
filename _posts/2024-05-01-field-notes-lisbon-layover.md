Lisbon wakes before the sun with the smell of espresso drifting out of pastelarias. For digital nomads, it’s a sweet spot between ocean air and strong infrastructure.

## Morning rhythm
- **Workspace:** Village Underground Lisbon
- **Why it works:** reliable 400 Mbps up/down, quiet focus pods, on-site espresso
- **Soundtrack:** lo-fi Lusophone beats + the occasional tram bell

## Midday recharge
The Time Out Market is five minutes away. Grab a bowl from *Asia Lab* and eat along the Tagus. Power banks stay full with abundant outlets.

## Sunset sign-off
End the day miradouro-hopping. We closed laptops on the rooftop of Park Bar, trading task lists for views.
