# About Fold & Ride

Fold & Ride is being assembled as a friendly launchpad for digital nomads who want fast answers and reliable planning support. The MVP focuses on outlining the key tools, guides, and community pathways that will grow into the full experience.

## What to Expect

- A toolkit of calculators that simplify budgeting, visas, and sustainability choices.
- Guides written for remote workers who need actionable, trustworthy information.
- A light community pathway for travellers to swap resources and stay connected.

## What's Next

This page will evolve alongside real data connections and field feedback. For now, it documents the intent behind the project so contributors understand the direction before we wire in production services.
