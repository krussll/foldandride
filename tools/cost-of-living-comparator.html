Cost of Living Comparator

The comparator will help nomads review how everyday expenses stack up between two locations. The MVP experience will keep inputs lightweight so testing can focus on accuracy and clarity.

MVP Notes

  • Inputs: origin city, destination city, lifestyle presets, and currency.
  • Outputs: monthly totals grouped by housing, groceries, dining out, and workspace fees.
  • Data sources: Numbeo for baseline figures with manual overrides where gaps exist.

During early QA, this content block will be replaced with a live comparison table fed by the backend service.
