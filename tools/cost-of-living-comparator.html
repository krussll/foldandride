Snapshot

The Cost of Living Comparator surfaces key spending categories so nomads can weigh the realities of moving from one city to another. It is built for fast side-by-side checks that clarify how rent, food, and workspace costs stack up.

Inputs

  • Origin and destination cities
  • Team size or household composition
  • Monthly rent or coworking budget caps
  • Lifestyle emphasis (budget, balanced, premium)
  • Preferred currency for the output

Insights

  • Headline monthly totals for both cities
  • Percentage difference by category with quick callouts
  • Suggested savings buffer for the pricier city
  • Key local notes (visa limits, taxes, or utility costs)
  • Downloadable comparison sheet for team planning

Research Backing

Data will pull from Numbeo, Nomad List, and partner surveys. MVP content leans on pre-curated tables so we can ship quickly while backend integrations take shape.

Roadmap Highlights

  • Layer in seasonal adjustments for heating, cooling, or tourist spikes
  • Introduce charts showing cost deltas over time
  • Allow saving favourite matchups to a logged-in dashboard
