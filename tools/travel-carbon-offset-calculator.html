Travel Carbon Offset Calculator

This calculator will translate flights, overland journeys, and accommodation choices into estimated emissions. The MVP keeps the workflow simple so users can quickly understand their impact.

MVP Notes

  • Inputs: transport mode, distance or route, cabin class, and trip frequency.
  • Outputs: CO₂e totals with suggested offset projects and estimated costs.
  • Data sources: Atmosfair API and internal conversion tables.

Once the backend service is in place, this page will render dynamic charts and calls-to-action that help travellers act on the results.
