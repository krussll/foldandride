Carbon Planning

Estimate, understand, and offset your travel emissions

Enter your journey details to instantly project the carbon impact of your next flight, rail adventure, or road trip. The calculator spotlights the biggest emission drivers and connects you with curated offset ideas.

  • Works with flights, rail, bus, car, and ferry journeys.
  • Swap between auto-calculated distances or your own custom mileage.
  • See per-passenger totals and quick-win reduction tips.

Step 1

Choose your mode of travel

Start with the biggest driver of emissions. Pick the transport mode you plan to take and we’ll unlock the rest of the journey details.

Different emission factors are applied for each mode.

You can adjust the mode later if your plans change.

Mode selected. Scroll down to refine the rest of your trip.

Step 2: Add trip details

Provide as much detail as you know. The calculator supports single trips and can estimate distances from common origin and destination pairs.

Use IATA, station codes, or city names for best accuracy.

Leave origin and destination blank to enter a custom distance below.

If provided, this value overrides the route lookup.

Include all travellers you are offsetting for.

Results update instantly when new data is available.
