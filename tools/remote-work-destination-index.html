Remote Work Destination Index

The destination index will highlight cities that balance affordability, connectivity, and wellbeing. The MVP focuses on a sortable list that surfaces headline metrics without overwhelming new users.

MVP Notes

  • Inputs: preferred climate, budget range, community size, and visa flexibility.
  • Outputs: ranked table or cards with internet speed, coworking density, safety, and cultural highlights.
  • Data sources: blended dataset from internal research and trusted public APIs.
