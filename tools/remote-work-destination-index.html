Overview

The Remote Work Destination Index blends quality-of-life signals with digital nomad essentials. It helps travellers find cities that balance affordability with community, safety, and fast internet.

Filters

  • Climate preference (tropical, temperate, seasonal)
  • Average monthly budget range
  • Minimum internet speed and reliability
  • Community vibe (quiet, balanced, vibrant)
  • Visa friendliness and length-of-stay requirements

Results

  • Ranked shortlist with overall Fold & Ride score
  • Category breakdowns (cost, internet, safety, community)
  • Key stats like coworking density and walkability
  • Seasonal callouts for weather or peak tourist months
  • Suggested itineraries or nearby hubs to pair

Data Approach

MVP content leans on curated research paired with public datasets. Once live, the index will pull from API partners and user surveys to refresh scores monthly.
