# Fold & Ride Toolkit

The toolkit gathers five core calculators designed around the first release of the platform. Each page outlines the purpose of the feature, the kind of inputs it will accept, and the early ideas for output formatting.

## Tools in Development

1. [Travel Budget Calculator]({{ '/tools/travel-budget-calculator/' | absolute_url }}) — Estimate baseline trip budgets by region, duration, and lifestyle.
2. [Cost of Living Comparator]({{ '/tools/cost-of-living-comparator/' | absolute_url }}) — Compare everyday costs between two destinations.
3. [Nomad Visa Finder]({{ '/tools/nomad-visa-finder/' | absolute_url }}) — Review eligibility snapshots for remote-work visa programs.
4. [Remote Work Destination Index]({{ '/tools/remote-work-destination-index/' | absolute_url }}) — Rank cities with filters for climate, connectivity, and community.
5. [Travel Carbon Offset Calculator]({{ '/tools/travel-carbon-offset-calculator/' | absolute_url }}) — Estimate emissions and match them to offset projects.

Each calculator page includes temporary copy blocks to help product and engineering teams sync on requirements before the live services are connected.
