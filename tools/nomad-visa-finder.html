Purpose

Remote workers have dozens of visa options to parse. The Nomad Visa Finder streamlines research with plain-language requirements and action items, letting travellers see where they stand in minutes rather than hours.

Inputs

  • Citizenship and residency status
  • Desired destination or region
  • Annual income band and proof-of-funds documents
  • Remote employer or freelance status
  • Travel timeline and length of stay

Outputs

  • List of matching visa programs with quick eligibility badges
  • Summary of key requirements, fees, and processing times
  • Link-out to official government or consulate sources
  • Checklist of documents to gather before applying
  • Alerts for limited-time pilot programs or caps

Compliance Considerations

Content references official immigration portals and will include disclaimers that regulations change quickly. The backend service will refresh data weekly to keep guidance accurate.

Future Enhancements

  • Localization of guidance for tax prep and mandatory insurance
  • Save-and-track feature to monitor status updates
  • Integration with partner legal teams for fast consultations
