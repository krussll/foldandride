What the MVP Delivers

The Travel Budget Calculator helps nomads sketch a realistic cost outline before they book. It combines core trip variables with lifestyle preferences so travellers understand how far their money will go in a new city.

Key Inputs

  • Destination region or city
  • Trip length in weeks or months
  • Preferred accommodation style and nightly rate band
  • Daily lifestyle slider (frugal, balanced, premium)
  • Team size or solo travel toggle

Projected Outputs

  • Total projected spend with a monthly roll-up
  • Breakdown of housing, workspace, transit, food, and lifestyle categories
  • Currency conversion into the traveller's home currency
  • Suggested savings buffer for emergencies
  • Downloadable summary for sharing with travel companions

Why It Matters

Planning long-term travel often leaves nomads guessing at monthly costs. This calculator anchors the conversation with vetted data sources so budgeting feels intentional rather than reactive.
